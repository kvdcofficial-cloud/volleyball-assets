About the

Foundation

The Volleyball Foundation, an initiative by the FIVB, was established in May 2024. The Foundation uses the global reach and power of volleyball to support community projects worldwide that have a positive impact.

Connect | Collaboration

Serve | Hands-on projects

Inspire | Advocacy

Within the Inspire pathway, advocacy and ambassadorship amplify our mission. Accomplished volleyball professionals serve as role models, bringing projects to life for grassroots players and communities. Backed by impactful data, communication strategies target the social impact sector, donors, and the public. Strategic media campaigns highlight volleyball's societal benefits, increasing visibility and reinforcing the Foundation's credibility. The ultimate goal is to integrate volleyball into broader social impact initiatives.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote grassroots volleyball development, empower youth, and create opportunities that build strong communities. Through organized programs, training, and support systems, we aim to make volleyball accessible to everyone.

  • Identify and nurture hidden talent in villages.
  • Support young athletes in developing competitive skills and sportsmanship.
  • Provide coaching, equipment, and exposure to players.
  • Strengthen community engagement through organized volleyball programs.
  • Organize rural, state, and national-level tournaments.
  • Collaborate with schools and local groups to establish volleyball learning pathways.
  • Promote health and well-being through physical activity.
  • Build infrastructure like courts and training centers in rural areas.
